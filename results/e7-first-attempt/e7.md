## E7 — Traffic spike with the autoscaler

Offered load steps 2 → 10 → 2 req/s (`spike` workload). The autoscaler (targets: 2 queued requests per worker,
TTFT p95 800 ms; 10 s sustained window to scale up, 30 s to scale down, min 2 / max 8) reads the routers' metrics;
scale-down drains a worker through its sidecar before stopping it.
